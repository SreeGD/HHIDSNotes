# The Greatest benefit is derived from association with elevated devotees

**His Holiness Indradyumna Swami** · Week 29 · Festivals and Kirtans · 45:50

## Summary

This is the second day of a seminar on the instructions of Prahlada Maharaja, continuing the discussion of a single verse of Srimad-Bhagavatam (SB 7.6.1), in which Prahlada teaches that an intelligent person should use the rare and temporary human form from the very start of life to practice devotional service, since even a small amount of sincere devotion yields full perfection. Indradyumna Swami establishes Prahlada as a bona fide instructor in the science of devotional service, explaining that we should hear both from eternally liberated souls (nitya-siddhas) and from those who reached perfection through practice (sadhana-siddhas), and that Rupa Goswami in the Upadeshamrita recommends associating with devotees of all three levels (kanistha, madhyama, uttama). He stresses that even association with a kanistha is precious and that hearing from a pure devotee (uttama-adhikari) carries special potency, comparing the pure devotee's words to nectar droplets that form in the sky and fall upon the audience, dissolving the conditioning of material life and awakening the conviction to abandon material existence and serve Krishna. The festival's theme is Vaishnava-seva (worship of and service to devotees), because through their association we gain in one lifetime the conviction, unattainable over millions of births, that there is no happiness in the material world; Prabhupada said 90 percent of our advancement depends on such association. He illustrates our stubborn material entanglement with the humorous analogy of endlessly trying to repair a broken microphone instead of simply taking a working one, and explains Maya's two potencies of first throwing the living entity into material entanglement and then covering it with illusion. Prahlada is shown to be a genuine tattva-darshi (seer of the truth) who actually beheld the Lord when Narasimhadeva appeared from the pillar solely to reciprocate Prahlada's love, illustrating that bhakti is reciprocal, Krishna rewarding each soul according to the quality of its love. Finally, comparing the choice of a spiritual guide to selecting a qualified doctor for the diseased soul, Indradyumna Swami congratulates the audience for joining ISKCON and says the sign of recovery from material disease is genuine happiness and joyful dancing in kirtan when one follows Prahlada Maharaja and the Guru-parampara.

## Key Points

- Prahlada's verse (SB 7.6.1) teaches that the rare, temporary human form should be used from earliest childhood for devotional service; Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakura once lectured on the very first Bhagavatam verse for three months in Dhaka, showing that a single verse contains unlimited transcendental knowledge.
- Following Rupa Goswami's Upadeshamrita, one should associate with devotees of all three levels (kanistha, madhyama, uttama); even a kanistha-adhikari, though not perfected, stands far above ordinary materialists because he knows the difference between matter and spirit and has chosen devotional service, and by association even kanisthas and madhyamas are quickly elevated.
- From the Krishna Book (The Source of Eternal Enjoyment), a pure devotee's speech acts like nectar droplets that form in the sky and fall upon the audience, so powerful that they destroy the conditioning of material life and awaken the conviction, unattainable over millions of births, to give up material existence and serve Krishna.
- The broken-microphone analogy (staying up all night, then a year later at the Odessa festival, still trying to fix the same dead mic instead of taking a working one) depicts our futile attempts to enjoy in the material world, like squeezing water from a stone; Maya has two potencies, to throw the soul into entanglement and then to cover it with illusion so it keeps thinking it can enjoy.
- The festival theme is Vaishnava-seva; by devotees' association faith (shraddha) is strengthened through sadhu-sanga, giving in one lifetime what millions of births could not, and Prabhupada said 90 percent of our advancement depends on association with devotees.
- A tattva-darshi (seer of truth) may be one who directly saw Krishna, or one who faithfully hears the message and transmits it unchanged, like a postman delivering a letter intact; thus even a kanistha or madhyama guru can deliver the Lord's message, and we also associate with the uttama Prabhupada through his book purports, which he called a manifestation of his ecstasy. (Story: when a journalist asked how he knew this knowledge, Prabhupada said, "Krishna personally told me all this.")
- Bhakti is reciprocal (Krishna rewards each soul as it surrenders); Krishna descended as Narasimhadeva from the pillar solely to reciprocate Prahlada's pure love and grant him darshan, and Prahlada is thus a genuine tattva-darshi and one of the twelve mahajanas. Choosing a spiritual teacher is like choosing a qualified doctor for the diseased soul, and the sign of recovery is real happiness and joyful kirtan when one follows Prahlada and the Guru-parampara.
