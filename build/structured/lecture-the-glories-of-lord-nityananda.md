## The Appearance Day of Lord Nityananda — Why the Lord Comes in Each Age

- We are sitting here in a mood of great celebration on a very significant occasion in our Vaishnava calendar: **the Appearance Day of Lord Nityananda.**
- In each age the Lord appears to propagate religion. **Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam (6.3.19): "dharmaṁ tu sākṣād bhagavat-praṇītam"** — the law of religion is enacted directly by God. No other individual can come to this world and tell us what is the appropriate means for understanding who we are, who God is, and what our relationship with God is. Only Krishna can do that. Therefore He comes regularly in each age.
- In each of the four ages — Satya, Treta, Dvapara, and Kali — He gives a slightly different process for coming to the stage of perfection.
  - **Why not one process for all ages?** Because people are different in each age. Just as you find people are different in different countries — all human beings, one in that sense, but each with its own unique culture, and what is good for one country may not apply to another — so the creation goes through different stages of spiritual evolution.
  - In the beginning of Satya Yuga, people are 99.9% God-conscious. In Treta Yuga, less God-conscious. By Dvapara Yuga, most people have lost it.

## The Goal of Life and the Onset of Kali Yuga

- Even Krishna states the goal in the Gita. **Bhagavad-gītā 7.3: "manuṣyāṇāṁ sahasreṣu kaścid yatati siddhaye…"** — "This is from God's mouth, so there is no challenging." Among millions and millions of men, perhaps one is interested in the science of self-realization; and of those who are interested (that original 1%), hardly one understands Him in truth. And this was the *previous* age, Dvapara Yuga.
- At the end of Dvapara Yuga, soon after Krishna spoke the Gita, He went back to the spiritual world — and that is when Kali Yuga began. **"We know exactly when Kali Yuga began. We have the answers to all questions, because we have a perfect source of knowledge."**
  - Anyone who creates something, maintains it, and can destroy it has perfect knowledge of it (a building, a company, whatever). Krishna created this material world, maintains it, and eventually the entire creation will become unmanifest and again manifest. He who does creation, maintenance, and destruction has perfect knowledge — so we don't have to speculate.

## The Nature of Kali Yuga — An Ocean of Inauspiciousness

- **Śukadeva Gosvāmī told Mahārāja Parīkṣit** at the beginning of this age that this age is an ocean of inauspiciousness (cf. **SB 12.3.51, "kaler doṣa-nidhe"**).
- "If you don't believe me, just pick up the newspaper. How much good news is there? At least when I was young, the back page was the good news. Now they don't even bother." An ocean of inauspiciousness.
- **But by God's grace, we may forget God, but He never forgets us.** We may turn our back on God and see only the shadow of material existence, but He never turns His back on us. He is in our hearts. As we travel through the 8,400,000 species of life, the Lord remains with us in the heart of every creature we enter. He is our ever-well-wisher.

## The Yuga-Dharma of Kali: Chanting the Holy Name, Given by Mahaprabhu

- Even for those born in this age there is a process of dharma: **kīrtanād eva** — just by chanting the name of Krishna, this mantra, **Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna…** (cf. **SB 12.3.51, "kīrtanād eva kṛṣṇasya… paraṁ vrajet"**).
- The chanting of Hare Krishna has always been around; there are references to chanting God's name in Kali Yuga, even a reference to the Maha-mantra itself. But **Chaitanya Mahaprabhu — the incarnation of Krishna in this age — is the authority, the boss; what the boss says stands.** He gave this mantra as the means of deliverance. He received the mantra from His guru, **Ishvara Puri.**
- Vaishnavas existed before Mahaprabhu came — many living in Vrindavan and everywhere; some knew the secret. But in those days, when a guru initiated a disciple, he would give some particular Vaishnava mantra or name of Krishna/Vishnu.
  - When Chaitanya Mahaprabhu appeared, He gave *the* mantra that all Vaishnava gurus should give their disciples in the age of Kali. So for 500 years in the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition, every guru gives his disciple the same Maha-mantra — **Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare…** Previously the guru gave a particular combination of names of God; by chanting that name one is purified because God is not different from His name. But now everyone in the Gaudiya tradition gets the same Maha-mantra.

## The Pancha-Tattva — God Comes in Five Forms in Kali Yuga

- Generally when Krishna comes, He comes in a singular form: Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Nrisimha, Rama, or Krishna Himself — **svayam bhagavān, avatārī,** the source of all incarnations.
- But in Kali Yuga, Krishna does not come in one singular form. **It is very esoteric — "most devotees, it kind of goes over their heads."** In this age God comes in five forms (a chapter in the Chaitanya Charitamrita; cf. **CC Ādi 7.6, "pañca-tattvātmakaṁ kṛṣṇam"**). "Just to cover all the bases," He comes as: a devotee, His expansion, an incarnation, the internal potency, and the marginal energy.
  - **Lord Chaitanya** — the devotee incarnation.
  - **Lord Nityananda** (whose appearance day it is today) — the expansion / expansion incarnation.
  - **Advaita** — the incarnation.
  - **Gadadhara** — the internal potency, Srimati Radharani.
  - **Srivasa Thakura** — the marginal energy.
- This evening we focus on one of those five aspects: **Lord Nityananda,** the expansion.

## Krishna's Opulence of Expansion — and How Nityananda Differs from Yogic Powers

- One of Krishna's opulences is that He can expand Himself; He has mystic powers.
- A yogi with siddhi can expand himself into twelve forms — "Wow!" — but all twelve forms do exactly the same thing at the same time: all raise an arm, all step, all sleep at the same time. "Voila."
- **Krishna can expand into unlimited forms, and each form can do something totally different.**
  - We are not practicing the process for getting siddhis / mystic powers, because we consider them child's play — "it's an adult game," still dealing with the material energy (becoming smaller than the smallest, bigger than the biggest, heavier than the heaviest, walking on water). It is a distraction from the love of God, though it is there inside you and yoga may reveal it.
- **Dwaraka example:** Krishna's principal domains are Vrindavan, Mathura, Dwaraka. In Dwaraka He is a king, and kings have many wives — Krishna has 16,108 wives. (A long story: these girls were kidnapped, and Krishna the hero took them all in, married them all, and built a palace for each queen, with ten children by each.)
  - When **Narada Muni** visited Dwaraka, he found Krishna present in each palace doing something different: playing chess with a son in one, eating dinner with a wife in another, being "henpecked" (Prabhupada uses that word) by a wife in another. "So Krishna's amazing. He can expand."
- **The test for false gods:** Even today in India we meet people who say they are God. "Yes sir, okay, I'll take your word for it — please expand into 16,108 forms." Sometimes they say, "I have done it but I have not given you the eyes to see," like Krishna gave Arjuna eyes to see His universal form; "you're inimical to me and I'm not giving you the eyes." Then: "Lift the Govardhana Hill — that Krishna can do." No mind, not even a computer, can determine how many forms Krishna can expand into for His pleasure, and some of those forms come here to the earth planet.

## Balarama, the First Expansion — and the Brothers Who Come Together

- Krishna's very first expansion is **Lord Balaram.** Prabhupada has stated that as the first expansion there is **no difference** between Krishna and Balaram — the only difference is color: **Krishna is darkish like a monsoon cloud, Balaram is white like an autumn cloud.** So little difference that they are sometimes called brothers in the scriptures, though they are so similar.
- As brothers, sometimes the "brothers" come together in various incarnations. 5,000 years ago Krishna and Balaram came together, with so many pastimes in Sri Vrindavan Dham — especially in **Raman Reti,** the soft sands where the cowherd boys wrestled and played.
  - This is why our **Krishna-Balaram Mandir** in Vrindavan places Krishna and Balaram in the center altar. Although there were many temples, Prabhupada said, "Why not another temple? I'm also a devotee; I can worship my Lord in the holy land of Vrindavan" — and put Krishna and Balaram in the center "because that's their place."
- The same brothers expand for other pastimes: **Rama and Lakshmana** (the Ramayana) are the same Krishna and Balaram. And **in this age those brothers come again as Lord Chaitanya and Lord Nityananda.**

## How We Know: Guru, Sadhu, Shastra — Narottama Dasa Thakura's Testimony

- How do we know Gora-Nitai are Krishna and Balaram? Because **Narottama Dasa Thakura tells us so.** Just as the Christians sing, "Jesus loves me, this I know, because the Bible tells me so," our authority is **guru, shastra, and sadhu.**
- **Who is the sadhu here?** Narottama Dasa Thakura — one of our heroes — with Srinivasa Acharya and Shyamananda were the original second generation who carried on the Sankirtana movement after the departure of the Pancha-Tattva. Narottama was a very bold preacher and a deep *rasika* devotee, "one of my heroes."
  - Prabhupada wanted us both to spread the Sankirtana movement *and* to become pure devotees — so we have to find the balance between **sadhana** and **sadhya** (achieving the goal of pure devotional service and serving the Sankirtana movement of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu). It is a delicate balance, and we need the unique guru who can teach us to do both.
- **Narottama Dasa Thakura's verse:** *"Brajendra-nandana yei, śacī-suta hoilo sei, balarāma hoilo nitāi"* — **Lord Krishna, the prince of Braja, has appeared as Lord Chaitanya, the son of Sachidevi, and Lord Balaram has appeared as Nitai.**

## What Do These Two Brothers Do? — Kaviraj Goswami's Sun-and-Moon Verse

- We know what Rama and Lakshmana did (the Ramayana, which we can read our whole lives) and what Krishna and Balaram did. But what did these two brothers, Gora-Nitai, do? For that we listen to **Krishnadasa Kaviraja Goswami.**
- **Chaitanya Charitamrita (Ādi 1.2):** "I offer my respectful obeisances unto Lord Chaitanya and Lord Nityananda, **who are like the sun and the moon.** They have arisen simultaneously on the horizon of Gauda to dissipate the darkness of ignorance and thus wonderfully bestow benediction upon all."
  - **"Upon all"** means you and me — anyone who has taken birth after the departure of Krishna 5,000 years ago until the **Kalki Avatara** comes at the end of 428,000 years from now. These two brothers have come to bestow mercy on anyone unfortunate enough to take birth between these two avatars.
  - At the end, in Kali Yuga, there is no more mercy. "A man convinced against his will is of the same opinion still." Mahaprabhu and His representatives will try their best to convince people, but some are so stubborn they will never be convinced — so for them there is the sword: the Kalki avatar comes and "chop, chop, chop," and by the hand of the Lord you are delivered. **"I would rather dance in ecstasy than get the sword on my head — I don't know about you."**

## The Special Quality of the Kali-Yuga Avatars — Bringing Us Onto the Stage

- Just as Balaram assisted Krishna and Lakshmana assisted Rama, in Kali Yuga **Nityananda assists Lord Chaitanya.** But there is a special quality to Nityananda's service that did not exist in the earlier pastimes.
- **The difference:** When Krishna and Rama came to earth, they enjoyed pastimes of love with their devotees but **did not invite the general people to participate** — it was "like a stage show." Goloka Vrindavan descended as Gokula Vrindavan, so wonderful, but the conditioned souls were only *witnesses*; they didn't have the *adhikāra* (qualification) to join. Like an RSVP function — "anybody can't crash the party."
  - Krishna showed the beauty, excellence, and mood of the spiritual world to give us the desire to attain it, and left His instructions — **Bhagavad-gītā 18.66: "sarva-dharmān parityajya mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja"** — just surrender to Me, start practicing bhakti-yoga. But the conditioned souls weren't performing those pastimes with the Lord. Those who came with the Lord (the pure devotees) were "on the stage," not you and me. "If you and me were there, we wouldn't be here now." Same with Rama and Lakshmana.
- **But in Gora-lila, Lord Chaitanya and Nityananda bring the conditioned souls into their pastime.** As the instigator of the yuga-dharma, Mahaprabhu's pastime was: He would leave His home every day, go into the district of Nadia, and have kirtan.
  - How did Krishna introduce dharma in Dvapara Yuga? By speaking Bhagavad-gita. How did Chaitanya and Nityananda introduce dharma for us (they don't need the dharma)? They went out and invited the people to chant with them.
- **The same is going on today.** When you go on harinama-sankirtana, it's not a show — you're praying that the people will come to the temple and chant and dance like you do before the deities.

## The Simplicity and Power of Sankirtana

- What is the effect of chanting, dancing, and taking prasadam in Kali Yuga? **Perfection.** Just singing, just dancing, having some *katha,* taking some prasadam — "that's all that's required." **kīrtanād eva** — "there's one good thing left in this age, and that's this process of sankirtan."
- **Sridhara Swami,** the original commentator on the Srimad-Bhagavatam, writes that if one just stays a devotee his whole life, follows the regulative principles, and chants the names of God, he is a candidate for going back to Vaikuntha. "Why should it be complicated?"
- Material life is characterized by being complicated — "so complicated that the solutions to the problems make the problems more complicated. There's no solution; it just gets worse and worse." But as soon as you agree to follow these simple regulative principles — **the "principles of freedom," as Krishna calls them in the Gita** — and you chant Hare Krishna, your life becomes sublime.
  - Prabhupada would say, **"Chant Hare Krishna and your life will be sublime."** Sometime in the 1970s or 80s somebody said, "Chant Hare Krishna and be happy." But there's a difference between happiness and sublimity — sublime is much deeper, indicating something on the transcendental platform, more than happiness. And what's the proof? **"You look in the mirror."**

## Not "Show and Tell" — Come Up on the Stage

- The difference between these two brothers in Kali Yuga: it's not show-and-tell; it's "Come on, all the audience, come up on the stage."
- **Poland festival analogy:** Sometimes at our festivals in Poland we have a final kirtan and the people are dancing; if it starts to rain, "I come up on the stage and then the kirtan really takes off, because the people want to be on the stage." It's human nature. They come onto the stage — but they come very close to that **gura-shakti,** the internal potency of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu.
- There is an internal, eternal potency — **the Daivi Shakti** — whom **Bhaktivinoda Thakura** worships as the internal potency of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu. We are used to associating with the external potency — "it gets boring after a while; there's no life." The internal potency you connect with by chanting **Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna…**

## Nityananda — The One Who Goes Out to the Masses

- The person who actually goes out, distributes, and brings people into the kirtan — **that's Nitai.** Mahaprabhu gave him that service. "We're all begging for service; if you beg it and you're sincere, you will get that service." Nitai had this desire, this mood of compassion.
- **Mahaprabhu preached to the great figures:** to **Prakashananda Saraswati,** leader of the sannyasis; to the king in Puri; to the head priest of the Jagannath temple, **Sarvabhauma Bhattacharya.** But who went out among the masses "to press the flesh" (like modern politicians who shake hands to win support)? **That was Nitai.**

## Vrindavan Dasa Thakura's Glorification of Nityananda

- In **Chaitanya Bhagavata, Vrindavan Dasa Thakura** glorifies Lord Nityananda:
  - He is **Tattva-simha** — "the very limit of absolute truth."
  - He is **Purna-shakti** — "the total potency of God."
  - He is **Seva-vigraha** — "the embodiment of service."
  - He is **Murti-mana Krishna-prema** — "the very embodiment of love of God."
- **We need the example.** There is *precept* (the philosophy) and there is *example* (the people who embody it). There is the Bhagavata (the book) — and the devotee is the *bhagavatam.* The philosophy is there, but we need someone practicing and perfecting it, "showing you what happens when you chant Hare Krishna for many lifetimes — tears come to your eyes, your voice falters, you fall down on the ground in ecstasy." We need to see that, or at least the different stages of getting there (**ruchi, asakti**).
  - We saw that on Srila Prabhupada in very recent times, and we will see it in the members of this movement as time goes on. **"Do not doubt that you will see those symptoms manifest in devotees. Sometimes it takes a couple of births, so just be patient."**

## The Historical Appearance of Nityananda in Ekachakra (1473)

- This is not just some fable — Nitai actually appeared in this world; Balarama took birth on the earth planet in a village called **Ekachakra in 1473.**
- His parents were unable to conceive a child. But one night a great sage appeared in the dream of Nitai's future mother, **Padmavati,** saying: "Mataji, do not worry. Very soon you will have a son who will be a **maha-purusha.** He will possess unlimited **shakti** and will deliver billions from bondage." — **"That's you; you're in the prediction."**
- **He was not an ordinary child.** Once He was playing with His friends, enacting the Ramayana (as children did in Vedic culture — "not now; they play all kinds of video games"). Nitai played the part of **Lakshmana** — "why not, he was Lakshmana previously."
  - In the play, one of Ravana's cohorts "shot" Lakshmana — but with a flower (not an arrow), and it fell to the ground. Another boy pretended to go to the Himalayas to fetch the **Sanjivani** herb like **Hanuman** did, brought it back, and "fixed" Lakshmana. This boy got something from mommy's cabinet — some sugar sweet — and **Nityananda came back to life.** People watching thought how much it resembled the Ramayana — "little do they know that this boy *was* in the Ramayana."

## Nityananda Was Twelve When Mahaprabhu Appeared — and His Roar

- When He was 12 years old, Lord Chaitanya appeared. Just as Balaram was a little older than Krishna, in this pastime Nityananda is a little older than Chaitanya Mahaprabhu — twelve years old, to be exact.
- When Mahaprabhu appeared at **Gaura Purnima** under the Neem tree in the house of **Jagannatha Mishra** and **Sachi Mata**, Nityananda was already 12 years old.
- **Haridasa Thakura** and **Advaita Acharya** knew of the appearance of Gauranga by their mystic power; they started loudly chanting, and people said, "What are you chanting?" — it was in code.
- But when Lord Nityananda sensed by His mystic powers that Mahaprabhu had appeared, **He roared so loud that the entire universe became unconscious** — from the highest planet down to the lowest (**A-brahma-bhuvanāt**). Brahma, the demigods, the Gandharvas, the humans, the snakes, the hellish beings from the lower planets — everybody collapsed because of the roar of Nityananda. He was so happy that His brother Krishna had appeared as Chaitanya Mahaprabhu.
- Nityananda was thinking: "I'm only 12 years old — how am I going to meet my brother? We were intimately connected in Krishna-lila and in Ram-lila. I'm here in Ekachakra and he's in Navadvipa." (It's only a four-hour drive, "but for him it was eternity.") "How will I ever leave Ekachakra? My mom and dad have so much affection for me; they won't let me go."

## The Sannyasi Who Asked for the Boy

- At that age, one sannyasi came to their house one day — "because that's what sannyasis do when they're hungry: they go to the house and stay for two months and are fed well. That's not the main reason they're there — but, yeah, they have the best prasadam in the universe." (Nitai's mother was the best cook.)
- The sannyasi came on the pretext of begging some milk (he could have milked a cow in the field, but sannyasis go to the houses of poor householders to give them spiritual wisdom). In return, the householders are meant to ask, "Is there anything we can give you in gratitude for this divine wisdom?" — and actually you are supposed to give a sannyasi *anything he wants,* "so be careful."
- This sannyasi looked at the boy and said, **"Give me your son. I need him as a servant to travel with me."** The mother collapsed; the father was in shock. But this is the time of dharma.

### Digression — "Where Have All the Flowers Gone?"

- "Where's dharma gone?" In the speaker's youth there was a song sung by **Bob Dylan** and joined by us: *"Where have all the flowers gone… young girls picked them every one… when will they ever learn?"* The next verse is about soldiers — *"where have all the soldiers gone… cannibals took them all… when will the politicians ever learn that war is not the solution?"*
- Where has dharma gone? People don't act dharmically anymore.
  - The speaker met some ISKCON youth: "Oh, you're devotees?" — "Yeah, we follow one principle: we're vegetarian." Where did dharma go?
  - Visiting a house in Delhi recently, he asked a boy, "Who's your favorite character in the Ramayana?" The boy said, "What's Ramayana?" "You don't know Hanuman?" "Is he a cricket player?" Where's dharma gone?

### The Deity of Murlidhar and the Mother's Surrender

- The mother did not agree: "You can't take my life from me." But the father consented, and this is the time of dharma when mystical things happen. "You have to give him anything he wants; can't say no."
- The sannyasi had a bag, and in the bag was a **deity of Murlidhar** (Krishna playing the flute). He gave it to Nitai's mother and said: **"You serve this deity; He will fulfill all your desires. In fact, one day you will see your own son in this deity."**
- She took the deity, and while looking at Him with loving eyes, **she saw the face of Nitai in the deity.** She said, "Take my son." Her heart was satisfied.
  - "Can you imagine giving your son to a traveling sannyasi?" But these are mystical things, and she was a dharmic woman: "If this is your desire, sir — you're a sadhu — by fulfilling your desires all my desires will be fulfilled. Because Krishna says one who says he's My devotee is not My devotee, but one who says he's the devotee of My devotee, he's My devotee." By serving the sadhus we get Krishna's mercy.
  - She gave the thing dearest to her heart — but she achieved the thing most dear to her heart: **she got God.** Of course her son *was* God, but now she knew it, having seen her son's face; the deity revealed himself to that pious woman.
- **Prabhupada said one time: "If you serve the deity with love and devotion, one day that deity will talk to you."**

## Nityananda's Travels and Initiation

- What would a 12-year-old boy want? "How many 12-year-old boys would like to travel with me in a few days — you want to continue school? Parents, close your ears — who wants to?"
- After traveling with that sannyasi for some time, the sannyasi passed away, and Nitai kept traveling.
- **Bhakti-Ratnakara** mentions that while visiting **Pandharpur** (where they have the deity of **Vithal** — "I was there some months ago"), He met the guru of Madhavendra Puri, **Lakshmipati,** and took initiation from him.
- This is the example: whatever a great person does, the common people follow. So God sets the example — **Mahaprabhu takes initiation from Ishvara Puri; Nitai takes initiation from Lakshmipati.**
- What happened? **He became a madman** — not influenced by the mundane material energy, but mad with **prema,** love of God — and He continued traveling.

## Wandering Through Braja — and Arriving in Navadvipa

- Traveling in India is allowed. In the early days (1972), when one of the speaker's god-brothers came to Vrindavan, he asked what to do there. **Prabhu said: "Just wander through Braja, because every grain of dust in Braja is Chintamani. There are 60 billion holy places in Braja-bhumi, and unlimited pastimes of the Lord are performed. You just wander."**
  - In the beginning Prabhupada didn't want us to wander so much, because as neophytes we might be influenced by improper philosophies. But nowadays we wander and find so many nectarean places and take the devotees there in our **Kartik parikrama.** ("In the next Kartik we have some super places we're going to take you — join us if you'd like.")
- So Nitai wandered and wandered and eventually wound up in **Navadvipa,** not far from Ekachakra (about 360 [km]). He was very satisfied there, because it was time for the two brothers to meet.
- As He entered **Navadvipa Dham** — which is not different from Vrindavan — **Vrindavan Dasa Thakura describes** that He was intoxicated with **Gora-prema,** swinging to and fro like a mad elephant, sometimes weeping, sometimes crying, sometimes laughing like a madman.
  - Being an **avadhuta,** He wore a blue dhoti, tulsi and rudraksha beads around His neck, one big earring in one ear, and ankle bells on His feet. "What kind of sadhu is that? Prabhu is the supreme enjoyer; no one can criticize; He is the reservoir of all pleasure; He can do as He likes."

## Lord Chaitanya's Longing — "Bring Me My Liquors"

- When Nitai arrived in Navadvipa, He went to the house of **Nandana Acharya** and took rest there.
- That night His brother Lord Chaitanya had a dream of Nithai and woke up in the morning **in the mood of Balaram,** and said to **Srivasa Pandita: "Bring me my liquors."**
  - Balaram gets intoxicated on the **Varuni** beverage — "a type of honey that comes from a tree. Everything is transcendental, so don't get confused." In Vrindavan there is nothing material; Varuni is not an intoxicant — everything intoxicates one more with love of God. Whatever one eats, whatever happens, everything increases the pastimes, especially **Madhurya-rasa** — all the rasas in Vrindavan are subservient to and accelerate the mood of Madhurya-rasa. "But that's another lecture."
- Srivasa said: "Lord, You have those liquors in the form of love of God, and You're already distributing them to others in the form of **Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare…**"
- Mahaprabhu said, "Okay, I agree — but to distribute Krishna-prema I require Nithai. Go find him. **Nithai, my transcendental associate every time I come to this world.**"

## Destiny's Time — "He Alone Can See Him Whom Lord Chaitanya Shows"

- **Haridasa Thakura** and **Srivasa Pandita** started looking for Nithai everywhere in Navadvipa — but it wasn't time. Destiny has its time; sometimes we're grateful to that time, sometimes disappointed. But time in the spiritual world is different: it is eternal, meaning nothing there is subject to destruction or deterioration; the same pastimes go on eternally, but ever-fresh. The rasa-lila, the marriage pastime — these go on and on, ever-fresh; there is no time in the spiritual world.
- It was not the time for Gora and Nitai to meet. **Vrindavan Dasa Thakura puts it very nicely in Chaitanya Bhagavata: "He alone can see him whom Lord Chaitanya shows."**
  - "Let's think about that for a minute." You cannot see Lord Nityananda without the grace of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, and you cannot see Chaitanya Mahaprabhu without the grace of Nityananda. You can see nothing without the grace of these personalities. Therefore **the spiritual master is taken as a representative of Lord Nityananda, or sometimes as Srimati Radharani.**
  - It is only by grace that we advance. We make our little effort — getting up in the morning, putting on tilaka — these facilitate, "but really our progress in consciousness comes by mercy, by the grace of Guru and Krishna." If you please Guru and Krishna by your **sadhana** and **seva** — especially by also bringing souls into the Krishna consciousness movement — "you'll see how quickly you make advancement."

## The Reunion of the Brothers

- Because it wasn't the time, Haridasa and Srivasa came back empty-handed. Then Lord Chaitanya said, **"Now is the time."**
- "Wouldn't you have liked to be there for the reunion of Krishna and Balaram, Ram and Lakshman, Lord Chaitanya and Nityananda meeting for the first time? How much bhava, how much prema, how much nectar would have been there? Where were we? What were we doing? Some senseless, stupid thing somewhere in material creation. Now is our chance."
- Mahaprabhu Himself, with His associates, went to the house of **Nandana Acharya, and they embraced.** "That would be the perfect movie — this is the original movie, the original love story. Vrindavan, Ayodhya, Dwaraka, Navadvipa — these are the original love stories; everything else is perverted."
  - Imagine Lord Nityananda walking in; Nityananda standing up; they run together and embrace; a flood of tears creates a puddle in the ground; and they fall down unconscious.

### Gadadhara Pandita Revives Them with a Verse

- To bring them back to consciousness, **Gadadhara Pandita recited the following verse — Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam (10th Canto — cited as Chapter 25, Text 5; the verse "barhāpīḍaṁ naṭa-vara-vapuḥ…," SB 10.21.5).** "This verse can also bring you back to your original consciousness."
- **The verse:** "Wearing a peacock-feather ornament upon His head, blue karnikara flowers on His ears, a yellow garment as brilliant as gold, and the Vaijayanti garland, Lord Krishna exhibited His transcendental form as the greatest of dancers as He entered the forest of Vrindavan, beautifying it with the marks of His footprints. He filled the holes of His flute with the nectar of His lips, and the cowherd boys sang His glories."
- ("Short intermission — did that bring you back to divine consciousness? If at first you don't succeed, try, try again.")

### Srivasa Manifests the Depth of Nityananda's Heart

- They came back to consciousness; the devotees were mesmerized, silent, nobody saying anything. So Lord Chaitanya requested **Srivasa** to break the silence by making the deep personality of Lord Nityananda manifest — the devotees hadn't met him yet, and Mahaprabhu wanted them to see the internal nature of this divine personality standing before them.
- Srivasa sang a verse from Srimad-Bhagavatam describing **Krishna coming back from the forest,** and on hearing it Nityananda fainted for a long time. No one knew what to do. Mahaprabhu said, "Chant Hare Krishna," and they had kirtan — **Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare…** — and Nityananda stood up. They embraced again.

### "I Have Found the Thief Who Stole My Heart"

- Then Lord Chaitanya said to the whole world (actually said it to Nityananda, but the whole world benefits): **"I searched the whole world over but could not find you. Now I have found the thief who has stolen my heart. Now I will never let you go."**
  - "Some man may say that to a woman — it churns the heart a little bit. But this should churn your transcendental heart, your heart of hearts." We're not interested in the physical heart, the muscle pumping oxygen; we're talking about the heart of hearts — **our love for Krishna.** It's there, covered by lust, anger, and greed. We want that heart to be awoken. "If you want a tattoo, get *that* tattoo — don't get some nonsense bulldog. I'm against tattoos, but I'm just saying: something significant."
- This is why the **Chaitanya Charitamrita** was the favorite book of **Bhaktisiddhanta Saraswati Thakura.** At that point the Sankirtana movement began in earnest, because the brothers were together — the team was together. "No man is an island unto himself. Things get done by teamwork; many hands make small work."

## The Two Brothers on Sankirtana

- These two personalities were "just so awesome, so cool." When they went on sankirtana (before Mahaprabhu took sannyas), they had long hair with flowers in it, earrings, nose studs, beautiful blue and golden cloth, garlands, rings on their toes — dancing and swirling. "People didn't see that stuff; sadhus would be 'om tat sat,' reciting verses. Here are these two personalities going to the villages, dancing like wild elephants, turning, dancing, tumbling."
- When their feet hit the ground, the dust would rise and be blown twenty kilometers away, settling on the heads of some ignorant farmers — and **the farmers would immediately experience the eight symptoms of ecstatic love of God.**
- The yuga-dharma, the Sankirtana movement: "Come chant with us — not watch, but come. You can be part of the pastime in Kali Yuga." Therefore the demigods are praying to take birth on this planet in Kali Yuga, because the opportunity for achieving perfection is greater than in any other age. **"So count your blessings, Prabhu, and don't procrastinate, because life is short and then you die. So take the chanting."**

## The Example of the Fifteen-Year-Old Girl

- "I met one 15-year-old girl today; she brought tears to my eyes — I didn't show it to her." That young lady is chanting **16 rounds every day.** She took a vow in Kartik to chant 16 rounds. "How many of you gentlemen, with your intelligence, chanted 16 rounds?" She chanted and couldn't stop, "because it's so sweet," even in school.
  - She is Donabhi's sister. "Where is she? Don't be shy — that's an order, you have to stand up. Give this girl a big round of applause. 16 rounds a day — she's got a head start on all of us."
  - "What kind of intelligence and taste has Krishna given that girl to realize the value of chanting Hare Krishna?" She is still chanting after Kartik (two months ago), and school is much harder now than it used to be ("what they're learning now, I was learning in university"). **So don't procrastinate — this is Kali Yuga.**
- Prabhupada said one time, "I'm just hoping some of my disciples will understand my message." And what is the message? What Shukadeva Goswami said 5,000 years ago: it's a **horrible place** — "I heard him say it three times: 'This is a horrible place. Don't come back.'" All you have to do is chant **Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare…**
  - That doesn't mean singing along with the kirtan tape in your car on the way to work. It means **sitting down** — like that young lady — before or in school and finishing 16 rounds of japa. "Krishna is watching; He's marking everything down in His little black book."
  - "Imagine if you're 15 what it will be like when she's 25, 35 — when she's my age. I started at 19; she's got a head start on all of us. Take that young girl's example to heart. If you're not chanting 16 rounds, this is a good day to begin — because this is the day of unlimited mercy."

## Nityananda's Mercy in Preaching to the Masses

- Nitai was just so merciful. He got the order from Chaitanya Mahaprabhu to go out to the masses, so He used to go out with **Haridasa Thakura** — they were Sankirtana partners. It was a little difficult for Haridasa (a sober **sadhaka,** the **Namacharya**), while Nitai was an **avadhuta,** Krishna Himself.
  - They'd knock on a door and Nitai would say, "Will you chant Hare Krishna?" "No, it's not our religion." "It's the **Yuga-Dharma** — I'm telling you, unless you chant Hare Krishna I'm going to come into your house, roll around, and break all your furniture until you start chanting." "You think you will?" "Yep" — and He'd come in, roll around, break things. "Okay, I'll chant Hare Krishna."
  - Sometimes the young girls would answer the door, and in the mood of Balaram and Krishna's transcendental love He would look at these beautiful "gopis" and start talking to them — "Gopis, can we have rasa-lila?" "You can't do that out here in society; they're not going to understand." Haridasa would report back, "I can't keep going out with him; he's the worst Sankirtana partner, causing all these problems." Sometimes He jumps in a lake and starts swimming on crocodiles "and all kinds of crazy stuff."

## The Net of Mercy — Jagai and Madhai

- Mahaprabhu wanted to show that if **Jagai and Madhai** can be delivered by the Maha-mantra, anyone can be delivered. If lions, tigers, bears, snakes, and monkeys can be delivered by Mahaprabhu going through the **Jharikhanda forest** chanting Hare Krishna, that means everyone, irregardless, is a candidate for Mahaprabhu's mercy.
- That is why **Kaviraj Goswami** said He made a net, like fishermen do, so no one can escape. Even Mayavadis — **Prakashananda Saraswati** and the 10,000 Mayavadis in Benares/Kashi — became devotees.
- Jagai and Madhai were really bad guys — rapists, extortionists; they burned people's houses down, burned people. But when they **expressed remorse** for what they had done (the first step) and agreed to chant Hare Krishna, Mahaprabhu embraced them.
  - **"All you need to do is express remorse — 'I will not do those bad things again' — and you agree to the positive alternative, to chant Hare Krishna. Mahaprabhu will accept you."**
- When Mahaprabhu embraced Jagai and Madhai, His golden form **turned black like night** — not black like Krishna (monsoon), but like night — because He had absorbed their sins. Then He said to the assembled devotees: **"If anyone ever criticizes a Vaishnava, I will give him these sinful reactions of Jagai and Madhai."**

### Warning Against Criticism of Devotees

- Criticism has become "the vogue" among some devotees in ISKCON now, especially on the internet — "you don't know who they are, what's their mentality, what they've done or not done, but they're a good writer; and you read, and you hear this nonsense, and then your faith is affected."
- We're warned over and over not to criticize or to hear criticism of devotees, but we're weak and we listen and our faith is affected. So Mahaprabhu said anyone who criticizes or hears this criticism, "I'll give you the sinful reactions of Jagai and Madhai."
- **Better to find the good qualities in devotees.** If there's something bad, have faith in the holy names: there is no accumulation of sins that can equal one name of Krishna. One name of Krishna, like the rising sun, can destroy all the darkness of night. So have faith in someone's chanting; whatever is bad there, by the mercy of the holy name it will go away, just as those bad things went away in your own heart. Better to see the good qualities.

### Madhai's Penance and Nitai's Fatherly Forgiveness

- Both were delivered, but **Madhai** took it hard, because he was the one who took the pot and smashed it on the head of Nitai. Nitai doesn't have blood, bones, arteries, or lymph nodes — but as a lila, to show, He exhibited the pastime of blood, and there was a lot of blood all over His shirt when Madhai hit Him.
- After being delivered, Madhai felt very bad and came to Lord Nitai to apologize. Nitai said, "That's okay. You just go to the bank of the Ganges and make a **ghat** there, and when people come to bathe, you wash their feet." Washing the feet of the pilgrims, Madhai became so humble — a very advanced devotee — "actually by the mercy of his guru," because Nitai was the one who desired his deliverance by taking up the process of chanting Hare Krishna.
- Still Madhai felt bad, and one day he came to Nitai and said: **"My Lord, when I think that it is You who sustains the universe in the form of Vishnu, that it is by Your Shakti that Brahma creates and destroys the universe, and that it is You whose body I struck — the fire of penitence begins to smolder in my heart and there is no peace for me. I think You could not have forgiven me. Please save me, my Lord."**
- Nitai said: **"Madai, you are like my own child, my son. If the child strikes the father, does the father feel hurt? But if you want, continue with this bathing ghat, and chant 200,000 names of Krishna every day, and continue to serve the lotus feet of anyone who comes here."** "What kind of punishment is that? 'Do your punishment: you chant Hare Krishna.'"

## An Appeal to the Heart, Not the Tired Body

- "This is the best use of our time. The body is tired, the body wants to go home and go to sleep. My secretary said, 'Maharaj, don't give a three-hour lecture' — but I'm appealing to your heart of hearts, not your tired body."
- "Yes, we can drive home and take a look at the news — see how Trump got acquitted and how the Democrats are having a hard time — and go to sleep and dream nonsense. But let's take 15 more minutes and hear another pastime before I stop."

## The Breaking of the Sannyasi's Danda

- Mahaprabhu took **sannyas** because in Vedic culture everyone would listen to a sannyasi; when He was a grihastha they weren't listening. He had His **danda,** and the first place He wanted to go was **Jagannath Puri** — described by Prabhupada as that holy place where his Guru Maharaj appeared, his Vyasa-Puja home.
- On the way, just before reaching Jagannath Puri, Mahaprabhu wanted to take darshan of a particular deity of Lord Shiva, so He said to Nitai, "You hold my danda, my sacred danda, while I go to the temple of Shiva, and I'll come back."
- As soon as Mahaprabhu disappeared, **Nitai broke that danda and threw it into the river.** That river became known as the **Danda-bhanga River,** having received the mercy of Lord Chaitanya's staff.
- When Lord Chaitanya came back — "Let's continue; where's my danda?" — Nitai said, "I broke that danda and threw it into the river." "What?! You broke my danda, the symbol of a sannyasi? Don't you know that the 33 crore/million demigods reside in the danda of a sannyasi?" Nitai said, **"I don't like the fact that you had to carry the demigods on your shoulder. They should carry you."** So Mahaprabhu said, "I'm going to Puri alone," and left.
- **The deeper understanding (per the Acharyas):** Within the danda of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu was more than the 33 million demigods — there was liquid prema, Krishna-prema, the very prema He had come to distribute by the process of chanting Hare Krishna, and which He Himself had come to experience in His pastimes at Jagannath Puri.
  - Krishna wanted to understand the love that Radha has for Him — she has this **Mahabhava** which no other living entity has, but because He is the object of that love He can't appreciate it. So He appeared in the mood of Srimati Radharani to experience it. In Puri, with His devotees, they would read verses from the Bhagavatam that brought forth this bhakti, this mood of the love of the residents of Vrindavan, particularly Radharani, and this way Krishna as Chaitanya Mahaprabhu experienced that love.
  - The Acharyas say that when Nitai broke open that staff, that's where that prema was, and by breaking it open it symbolized that Chaitanya Mahaprabhu was showering the whole world with that love — not the awe-and-reverence love of Vaikuntha, but **Vraja-prema,** the intimate, fun love of Vrindavan (the playground of God) — now flooding the earth by the mercy of Nitai.

## Nityananda as the Example for Householders

- When Mahaprabhu came back from South India, He asked Lord Nityananda to become a **grihastha** — interesting, because most of Lord Chaitanya's associates were renunciates, so one might think one has to renounce to achieve perfection.
- But again, **Narottama says:** whether one is a sannyasi or a householder "with ten children and a big bank balance and driving a Mercedes," if that person within his heart wants to serve the Sankirtana movement of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu — "I want his association; I take the dust of his feet upon my head." He can be a renunciate or a householder, but if he has that desire to serve Chaitanya, "I want that person's association."
- To show how to live the **grihastha** life — it's not easy; it's a challenge to remain Krishna conscious, to inspire each other, to do Krishna conscious things together: chant your rounds together, take prasadam together, read Shastra together, go to the temple together, go on Vandan[a]. **These things make it grihastha life and not "grihamedhi" life.** Time is of the essence, but we have to find the time to do these things with our families and children.
- So **Nithai got married.** Lord Chaitanya Mahaprabhu became the crest jewel for those in the renounced order of life, and His half-brother became the example for all householders.

## Where Lord Chaitanya Always Resides — Dance in Kirtan

- From all these pastimes we understand that Nithai was very dear to Lord Chaitanya. **Chaitanya Charitamrita says that Lord Chaitanya Mahaprabhu always resides in four places:**
  1. At **Mother Sachi's house.**
  2. At the **kirtan of Srivasa Pandita.**
  3. At the **home of Raghava Pandita.**
  4. **Wherever Lord Nityananda is dancing.**
- "Isn't that wonderful? So take every opportunity to dance in kirtan and be assured of the association. Don't just be wall-flowers." Even the speaker, "an old man, I'm seventy," doesn't sit on the benches when there's kirtan. **"Don't be wall-flowers, be lotus flowers — swim in the nectarean ocean of Krishna-bhakti."** Even the old guys — Advaita Acharya was the best example.

- One senior Vaishnava, renowned among Gaudiya Vaishnavas for distributing the glories of Lord Nityananda, told this true story in Detroit: **make many sets of Gaura-Nitai and distribute them house to house.** They lived in a poor township, mostly African-American. **Prabhupada said go door to door and distribute Gaura-Nitai deities** — "that's how merciful Gaura-Nitai are; we can't fathom it."
- To conclude, the speaker reads a letter from **Srila Prabhupada (January 27, 1970):**
  - *"Nityananda Prabhu approached Jagai and Madhai at the risk of being personally injured, and still He definitely delivered them. The world is full of Jagais and Madhais — namely drunkards, woman-hunters, meat-eaters, and gamblers — and we will have to approach them at the risk of injury, insult, and similar other rewards. To face such reverse conditions of life and to suffer thereby the results of actions is considered as the greatest penance and austerity in the matter of spiritual advancement of life."*
- **"Don't be afraid."** The speaker has gone out many times on Sankirtana/Harinam in the townships. Once you create that spiritual atmosphere through chanting **Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare,** "you're working under the divine umbrella of Krishna's protection."
  - Just begin by approaching your neighbors, or people in your business, or wherever — just say Hare Krishna, give them some prasadam, start to preach. Wherever there's preaching, there's all auspiciousness.
  - "Even tomorrow I'm going on **Food for Life.** I'm just at the end of my recovery from two months of getting better from my operations, and as a celebration I'm going out on Food for Life in an African township tomorrow — distributing prasadam and chanting through all the shanty towns. This is the best way to celebrate."
