## Opening: The Second Day on Prahlada Maharaja's Instructions

- This is the second day of a seminar on the theme of the instructions of Prahlada Maharaja. Today the speaker will continue on the same verse that was begun yesterday.
- We can speak about a single verse of Srimad-Bhagavatam for many days, months, or even years, because it is filled with such a great quantity of transcendental knowledge. Even if we spoke about one verse for many months, we could not fully convey its complete meaning.
- **Example:** Bhaktisiddhanta Saraswati Thakura lectured on the very first verse of Srimad-Bhagavatam in **Dhaka** (which was then part of Bengal and is now the capital of Bangladesh). He gave lectures on that first verse, in the temple there, for **three months** continuously — one verse.

---

## The Verse: SB 7.6.1 — Begin Devotional Service in Youth

- **"kaumara acaret prajno dharman bhagavatan iha / durlabham manusam janma tad apy adhruvam arthadam"** (*Srimad-Bhagavatam 7.6.1*).
- **Prahlada Maharaja said:** one who is sufficiently intelligent should use the human form of body from the very beginning of life — in other words, from the youngest years — to practice the activities of devotional service, giving up all other engagements.
- The human body is very rarely obtained, and even though it is rare, it is just as temporary as other bodies. Nevertheless it is very important, because in the human form of life a person can perform devotional service. **Even a small amount of sincere devotional service can help a person attain complete perfection.**

---

## Prahlada Maharaja as Our Instructor in Devotional Service

- Yesterday we accepted Prahlada Maharaja as an instructor in the science of devotional service.
- One may accept him as a **nitya-siddha**, since he is an eternally liberated devotee in Krishna consciousness. Or he may be accepted as one who became an instructor because he attained perfection by means of the standard process of devotional service — that is, one who was a **sadhaka**, who practiced devotional service and ultimately reached perfection.
- At the end of yesterday's class we concluded that we should hear from both types of personalities: those who are eternally liberated souls, and those who, through the process of devotional service, attained this level, having risen from the conditioned state.

### Associate With Devotees of All Three Classes (Nectar of Instruction)

- In the **Upadeshamrita** (*Nectar of Instruction*), Srila Rupa Goswami states that we should associate with devotees; but he also recommends associating with **advanced** devotees when possible.
- He describes three classes of devotees: **Kanistha, Madhyama, and Uttama.** Association with any of these categories of devotees is always desirable, because devotees are very special personalities.

### Do Not Neglect Even the Kanistha-Adhikari (BG 7.3)

- We should not neglect the opportunity to associate even with a **Kanistha-adhikari.** Although the Kanistha-adhikari is not on the highest level of perfection, he is much more advanced than an ordinary person:
  - He knows the difference between material and spiritual life.
  - He has consciously decided to give up material life and dedicate himself to devotional service to the Lord.
- This is a very rare opportunity. **Bhagavad-gita 7.3** — "manusyanam sahasresu kascid yatati siddhaye..." — Krishna says that out of many millions of people, perhaps one is interested in the science of self-realization; and out of many such elevated persons, hardly one truly knows Me. So such a soul, or Vaishnava, is very rare. Even though he is not yet an elevated devotee, he is nevertheless far more elevated than the general mass of people who are engaged in materialistic activity.

### The Kanistha Rises Through Association

- Through association with elevated devotees, one's faith in the process of devotional service gradually develops.
- Because of his youth and inexperience in devotional service, he may sometimes be unable to present serious arguments in favor of devotional service. But as his faith becomes firmer and his knowledge and understanding of devotional service increase, he gradually becomes an **Uttama-adhikari.**
- In the association of such advanced devotees, all the other Kanisthas and Madhyamas are very quickly elevated. Such is the nature of association with elevated devotees.

## The Special Potency of Hearing From a Pure Devotee

- Srila Prabhupada once said that chanting Hare Krishna always brings benefit. But if we hear the chanting of Hare Krishna from an **Uttama-adhikari**, a pure devotee, such chanting carries a special spiritual blessing.
- In his translation of the **Tenth Canto** of Srimad-Bhagavatam (the *Krishna book*), Prabhupada mentions that when a pure devotee speaks, his speaking acts like **little droplets that form in the sky and fall down as nectar upon the heads of the audience**, enlightening them in Krishna consciousness.
- Srila Prabhupada further explains in the *Krishna book* (*The Source of Eternal Delight*) that such association with an Uttama-adhikari is like drops of rain that fall like nectar upon the earth: these drops of nectar from the mouth of the pure devotee fall upon the conditioned souls.
- Because of the elevated position and deep realization of the pure devotee, his words are filled with such potency that they **destroy the conditioning of the souls** in the material world. Through this hearing, the conditioned souls develop a certain conviction: *"I will give up material life, and I will dedicate myself to devotional service to the Lord."*

### What Millions of Births Could Not Do, Association Does

- What has not happened to us over millions of years and millions of lives in the material world happens through hearing from pure devotees of the Lord.
- We can imagine: millions of births and deaths did not help us understand that this material world is a place of suffering, periodically repeated. We endured one failure after another in our attempts to enjoy in the material world, yet we never came to the conclusion that we should give it up.

### Analogy: The Broken Microphone

- Suppose tonight the speaker sat and tried to use a non-working microphone. He would ask one devotee, then another, to repair it; nothing would work; he would try to do it himself; nothing would come of it. He would keep trying all night until morning, and you would say, "Good morning!" Isn't it obvious that one should just take another, working microphone?
- He would sit the whole next day still trying to fix that same microphone; in the evening you would come and he would still be at it; you would come back the next year to the **Odessa festival**, and he would still be trying to repair the same microphone.
- It is a funny example, but really we are laughing at ourselves — because our position in the material world is exactly like this. There is no happiness here, yet we all keep trying somehow to get happiness in the material world. These attempts are as meaningless as trying to squeeze water out of a stone. Such is the nature and the influence of the material energy.

### Maya's Two Potencies

- Prabhupada said that **Maya-devi has two potencies**:
  1. To throw the living entity into the entanglement of material life.
  2. To cover the living entity with illusion.
- First she throws the living being into the material world, and then she covers him.
- (Aside: "I don't mind if the babies cry. I mind if the dogs bark." The babies may cry, but the dogs are not allowed to bark.)
- So Maya is covering us, and we keep thinking, "We can enjoy, we can enjoy, we can enjoy." We are not convinced even after millions of births. But through association with the Vaishnavas, the elevated devotees, we finally understand this fact — that in the material world there is no happiness. That is how powerful the association of pure devotees is.

## The Theme of the Festival: Vaishnava Seva

- What is the particular theme of this festival? The theme of this festival is **Vaishnava Seva.** *Seva* actually means **to worship.**
- So why do we worship the lotus feet of the Vaishnavas? There are so many reasons; this is one reason: **by their association we become convinced of the temporary nature of material life.**
- In association with them, we lose our faith in the material world, and we gain faith in the spiritual world. Through this association our faith (*shraddha*) is strengthened — through **sadhu-sanga**, association with elevated personalities.
- This result we do not achieve even after millions of lives. What we could not achieve over millions of lives, we achieve in a single life through association with devotees, with bhaktas. Therefore Prabhupada says that **90% of our advancement depends on association with devotees.**

## Hearing From One Who Has Seen the Truth (Tattva-darshi)

- Another reason we hear from Prahlada Maharaja is that he is a devotee of this level — a very elevated devotee.
- Yesterday we spoke about hearing from those who are **Tattva-darshi**, seers of the truth (**Bhagavad-gita 4.34** — "jnaninas tattva-darsinah"). We hear from those who know the truth.
- This can mean that such a person has literally **seen the Absolute Truth, Bhagavan Sri Krishna**; he may be directly connected with the Supreme Lord.

### Story: "Krishna Personally Told Me" (Prabhupada's Interview)

- Once a newspaper correspondent was interviewing Srila Prabhupada. He asked, "How do you understand all this knowledge that is there in these books you are presenting? Do you personally speak with God?"
- The devotee who was there helping at the interview — a GBC man — interrupted and said, "No, our spiritual master receives this knowledge from his guru; what is described in the scriptures and passed down through the spiritual masters of the past (the *sadhus*) is how he transmits this knowledge to us."
- But Srila Prabhupada stopped him, and surprised everyone with the following statement: **"No, Krishna personally told me all this."** The correspondent quickly wrote it down.
- And in fact this is confirmed: **Bhagavad-gita 10.10** — "tesam satata-yuktanam bhajatam priti-purvakam / dadami buddhi-yogam tam yena mam upayanti te" — those who serve Me with bhakti, with love — My intimate associates — I give them the understanding by which they can come to Me. This is *tattva-darshi.*

### Tattva-darshi Also Means One Who Hears and Acts

- But a tattva-darshi may also be one who **hears** this knowledge and acts on the basis of it. We spoke yesterday about how a saintly person perceives this world through **hearing, not through sight.** And we quoted Srila Prabhupada, who said, **"One who hears well can explain well."**
- So a tattva-darshi may be one who received this knowledge by hearing, reflected upon it, and acts on the basis of that knowledge; such a person is also considered a seer of the truth. And this may mean a **Kanistha or Madhyama.**

### One May Accept a Kanistha or Madhyama Guru (The Postman)

- Very interestingly, Srila Prabhupada says that one can accept a spiritual master who is on the level of Kanistha or Madhyama. How is this possible?
- Because Prabhupada said that such a devotee acts as **the Lord's representative** — the humble representative of the Lord — and he presents the message without distortion.
- He gives the example of the **postman**: the postman takes the letter, brings it from the post office, and delivers it to the address; it reaches the recipient without any change. He is simply delivering the message from A to B.
- So the spiritual master — whether on the level of Kanistha, Madhyama, or Uttama — is one who delivers the message of the Lord to his disciple **without change.**

### Yet Strive for the Association of an Uttama-Adhikari — Available Through Prabhupada's Books

- But again, Prabhupada says that if there is the opportunity to hear from a devotee on the level of **Uttama-adhikari**, one should strive for such an opportunity.
- And of course such an opportunity is open to all of us, because we have the opportunity to associate with Srila Prabhupada through the commentaries (purports) in his books. This is no less important an association.
- Prabhupada spent a very important part of his life — a great deal of time at the end of his life — on the commentaries in his books, and he once even described them as a manifestation of his **ecstasy**; he put his ecstasy into the commentaries. So we have the opportunity to associate with an Uttama-adhikari every time we open his books and read them.

## Prahlada Maharaja Is an Uttama-Adhikari Who Saw the Lord

- We also have the opportunity to associate with an Uttama-adhikari in the personality of **Prahlada Maharaja.** He was, obviously and without a doubt, *tattva-darshi.* No one should doubt that Prahlada Maharaja saw Krishna. Does anyone who has read this story have any doubt that Prahlada Maharaja was tattva-darshi — that he actually saw the Lord?
- Look at the special nature of his bhakti.

### The Nature of Bhakti Is Reciprocal (BG 4.11)

- What is the nature of bhakti? The nature of bhakti is that it is **reciprocal.**
- **Bhagavad-gita 4.11** — "ye yatha mam prapadyante tams tathaiva bhajamy aham" — all of them, as they surrender to Me, I reward them accordingly.
- What is the reward? What reward does a devotee want for his service? **No reward.** He simply wants the Lord's association. That is what he is calling out for every day: "Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna — give me Your association, give me Your service." In accordance with the level of your love, Krishna will reciprocate with you.

### We Desire the Association of Great Devotees — But Need Their Quality of Love

- Sometimes we may think: "It would be nice if, like **Hanuman**, I were embraced by Ramachandra. I would like the Lord to save me in a dangerous situation, like **Draupadi.** I might want the Lord to be my chariot driver, or my friend, like **Arjuna.**"
- We may desire this, but we do not have the qualification for it. That is the **Param-gati**, the ultimate goal. But to have that type of association with Krishna, you must have the quality of bhakti that those devotees had. That is how you gain that confidential association with the Lord — because of the quality of your love.
- Love is characterized by reciprocity; such is the nature of love. You serve someone, and that person wants to reciprocate with service. And these great devotees received such reciprocation from the Lord, because He responded to their love.

### The Bible's Example of God's Love

- As the Bible describes: **"For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, Jesus Christ."** We chose to turn away from the Lord, to leave His abode; but nevertheless the Lord did not turn away from us. He sent to us, with a message, His only begotten Son — called Jesus Christ — to bring us back to Himself. Such is the science of bhakti.

## The Lord Descended Only for Prahlada (BG 4.8)

- How much love did the Lord have for Prahlada Maharaja? Or, in other words, what was the quality of Prahlada Maharaja's love, because of which the Lord descended into this world?
- **"paritranaya sadhunam..."** (*Bhagavad-gita 4.8*), Krishna says. He says more in that verse, but let us focus on *paritranaya sadhunam.* He comes into this world to reciprocate with the love of His devotees.
- In this verse three reasons are given: the **liberation of the righteous**, the **punishment of the sinners**, and the **reestablishment of religious principles.**

### The Real Meaning of "Pious" — Sukriti (Visvanatha Chakravarti Thakura)

- **Visvanatha Chakravarti Thakura**, in his comments on the Bhagavad-gita, clarifies this word "pious." It does not mean a materially pious person, but one who has amassed so many devotional, spiritual credits as a result of devotional service.
- This is the real meaning of **Sukriti** — pious activity. Not mundane pious activity, but even **ajnata-sukriti**: even unknown/unknowing devotional service can move mountains.
- Even though **Ajamila** unknowingly began to chant the holy name of the Lord — though he meant to call his son — nevertheless he attained liberation as a result of it. We can only imagine what benefit a person receives who engages in **fully conscious** devotional service: one who fully understands that he is the servant of the Lord and that Krishna is God, and who tries to do everything possible to offer his service for the satisfaction of Krishna. This is what Krishna wants — acting in accordance with the definition of pure devotional service given in the **Bhakti-rasamrita-sindhu** of Srila Rupa Goswami.

### Nrsimhadeva Appeared Only for Prahlada's Sake

- Prahlada's devotion is so great that because of it the Lord came here and manifested a special incarnation, **only to liberate Prahlada** — He liberated no one else. (Of course, Hiranyakashipu also received liberation in parallel, but he went to the Brahmajyoti — that is not true liberation, but nevertheless.)
- And Nrsimhadeva did not remain on this planet, as other incarnations do, to reestablish religious principles. He actually came only for the sake of Prahlada; all of this incarnation, all of this *lila*, was manifested only for his sake. And afterward the Lord returned to the spiritual world. Because of this, Prahlada received the *darshan* of the Lord.

### The Lord Appeared From the Pillar

- The Lord appeared from a completely unexpected place — from a **pillar.** Hiranyakashipu had challenged Prahlada: "If your Lord is everywhere, show Him to me. Is He here, there — or in this pillar?" "Yes, He is there."
- So, by the "mercy" of Hiranyakashipu — the speaker corrects himself: not the mercy, but the **challenge** — of Hiranyakashipu to the Lord, the Lord appeared from the pillar.
- And so Prahlada is truly a **tattva-darshi.** By the "mercy" (in quotation marks) of Hiranyakashipu — by his challenge — Lord Nrsimhadeva manifested from the pillar. We can understand that Prahlada Maharaja is truth, *tattva-darshi.* He was able to see the deeply hidden truth.
- Undoubtedly he is deeply qualified to address us in these chapters of Srimad-Bhagavatam, because he received the darshan of Krishna Himself. Therefore he is a true Uttama-adhikari, and therefore he is listed as one of the **twelve Mahajanas.** And we should hear from him.

## Choosing the Right Spiritual Doctor

- So yesterday and today we have simply been establishing Prahlada Maharaja in the role of instructor of devotional service. Krishna consciousness is a very serious science.
- Just as, when you fall ill, you go to a doctor and your first question is, "Is he really a qualified doctor? Mom, Dad, is he a good doctor?" — when we fall ill we go to a doctor, and first we must make sure that he is really a good doctor who can cure us. We do not want to hand ourselves over to some unqualified surgeon to operate on us.
- What to speak, then, of the one who operates on the **diseased soul**? We can call it a disease of the soul — though the soul is not actually subject to any material influence, because of our identification [with matter] we can say we are in a very diseased condition. And we must be very careful about which **spiritual doctor** we entrust our life to. We must make sure that we surrender to the one to whom we ought to surrender.

### The Most Important Choice in Life

- The most important choice in life is to choose the right instructors — those who will lead us out of the material world into the spiritual world. This question is more important than which university I will study at.
- The speaker remembers, when he was a boy finishing school, that for him the most important question in life seemed to be: which university to enter? At that moment in life, this question seemed the most important. Which young man to marry? Which girl to marry? What career to build? These seem to be the most important questions in life.
- But these are not as important as the question of into whose hands to entrust your spiritual life — to which spiritual organization I should dedicate myself.

### Congratulations on the Right Choice

- The speaker congratulates everyone that they have made the right choice: they have joined the movement of the **International Society for Krishna Consciousness**, founded by Srila Prabhupada; and the predecessor of this movement was the great devotee **Prahlada Maharaja.**
- If we properly follow the instructions given by Prahlada Maharaja and our Guru Parampara, then our spiritual — our soul's — disease will pass very quickly.

## The Sign of Recovery: Happiness (BG 9.2, BG 2.59)

- A very simple sign of our recovery is the **feeling of happiness.** This is not just some sentimental happiness. This happiness arises because we give up the sources of suffering and come into contact with the source of pleasure. And this choice is based on a very firm philosophy.
- Therefore Lord Krishna says that Krishna consciousness, or **Bhagavata Dharma**, is a spiritual process performed in a very joyful way (**Bhagavad-gita 9.2** — "su-sukham kartum avyayam" — joyfully performed and everlasting). You will understand that you have been cured of the disease of material existence by the simple sign that you feel happy in Krishna consciousness.
- Mahaprabhu says that this process gives us a **taste** which we always crave. Krishna says that this higher taste in devotional service allows us to easily give up all material attachments (**Bhagavad-gita 2.59** — "param drstva nivartate" — the lower taste is given up upon experiencing a higher one).
- If you properly follow the instructions of Prahlada Maharaja and the Guru Parampara, you will joyfully dance in kirtan.
